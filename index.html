祁连山 · 河西走廊The Qilian Mountains and the Hexi Corridor

唐人颜师古注《汉书》：“祁连山即天山也，匈奴呼天为祁连。”天山之北，一串绿洲连成一条走廊。两千多年里，月氏、匈奴、汉、鲜卑、粟特、吐蕃、回鹘、党项、蒙古、藏、回、裕固……都从这里走过，都喝过这座山的雪水。

Glossing the History of the Han, the Tang scholar Yan Shigu wrote: “The Qilian are the Heavenly Mountains; the Xiongnu call heaven qilian.” North of them a chain of oases forms a corridor. For more than two thousand years Yuezhi, Xiongnu, Han, Xianbei, Sogdians, Tibetans, Uyghurs, Tanguts, Mongols, Hui and Yugurs have passed this way, and all of them drank this mountain’s snow.
